## 4. Data

- `data/sample_suppliers.json` and `data/calibration_portfolio.json` are fictional or synthetic and
  were created for this project; the calibration portfolio is generated by
  `examples/generate_calibration_dataset.py`.
- `src/supplier_risk_agent/providers/mock_data/wgi_mock.csv` contains **fictional** values for
  fictional regions. It is not World Bank data.
- No World Bank Worldwide Governance Indicators (WGI) data is included. If you download WGI data and
  load it with `WorldBankWgiProvider.from_csv()`, the World Bank's terms of use for that dataset
  apply; check them when you download.
